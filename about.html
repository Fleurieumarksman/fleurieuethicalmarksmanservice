About FEMS

Qualified. Experienced. Professional.

Specialist vertebrate pest animal control supported by field experience, formal qualifications, biological science and regulatory knowledge.

Fleurieu Ethical Marksman Service combines practical field experience, formal qualifications and specialist training relevant to vertebrate pest animal control.

Our lead marksman has extensive experience in humane euthanasia by firearm across a broad range of species and environments, supported by qualifications and experience in pest control activities, biological sciences, investigations, regulatory compliance and operational safety.

Our operating principles

Humane & Responsible

Animal welfare and humane outcomes are central to the way control activities are planned and undertaken.

Advanced Capability

Thermal imaging, night vision and specialised equipment can be used where appropriate to improve detection, identification and precision.

Conservation Focused

A strong understanding of biodiversity, habitat protection, native wildlife and the broader environmental effects of invasive species informs the way we assess each site.

Site-Specific Approach

Every property is assessed individually. Control methods are selected according to the species involved, the surrounding environment, safety considerations and the management objectives of the client.

Fully Insured

Fleurieu Ethical Marksman Service is fully insured, licensed and compliant with relevant South Australian requirements.

Specific control activities remain subject to applicable legislation, authorisations, site conditions and operational requirements.
